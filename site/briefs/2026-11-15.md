## Bottom line up front

Today's intelligence highlights a convergence of AI-driven automation in cyberattacks, with frameworks like BOSSAI and CyberStrikeAI targeting education sectors alongside critical infrastructure. Universities face dual threats from credential theft operations and sophisticated mobile malware campaigns exploiting supply chain compromises.

## Top threats

### 1. BOSSAI AI Mesh Targets Education and Emergency Services

*Findings: [F3](#f3)*

The BOSSAI campaign utilizes a mesh of 12 AI agents to automate attacks against universities, exfiltrating student data alongside medical records from emergency services.

**Recommended actions**
- Monitor for the identified IOC domains and IP ranges associated with the attacker-collector infrastructure.
- Review access controls for student databases and healthcare systems given the cross-sector targeting.
- Deploy behavioral analysis tools to detect automated reconnaissance patterns.

### 2. CyberStrikeAI Framework Targets Educational Institutions

*Findings: [F6](#f6)*

This open-source framework combines LLM orchestration with traditional C2 servers to execute full attack chains against educational institutions in Africa and Latin America.

**Recommended actions**
- Block the identified fastcdn\[.\]bar domain and associated IP addresses at the perimeter.
- Audit SQL Server infrastructure for unauthorized access points used as initial entry vectors.
- Implement network segmentation to limit lateral movement capabilities.

### 3. ClingSTUN Backdoor Exploits IoT and Public STUN Infrastructure

*Findings: [F2](#f2)*

This Linux malware strain exploits unpatched vulnerabilities in Internet-facing devices to establish persistent footholds, abusing public STUN infrastructure for connectivity.

**Recommended actions**
- Patch all identified CVEs listed in the finding immediately.
- Disable or restrict access to public STUN servers on internal networks.
- Scan for IoT devices running unpatched firmware.

### 4. KeyHunter Operation Targets Academic Credentials and LLM Keys

*Findings: [F1](#f1)*

The operation harvested API keys from misconfigured cloud infrastructure and created fraudulent student accounts at major universities.

**Recommended actions**
- Audit cloud configurations on Vercel, Netlify, and Railway for exposed API keys.
- Verify the integrity of student account creation processes against known fraud indicators.
- Monitor Telegram channels for automated scanning activity.

### 5. Critical Vulnerabilities Added to CISA KEV

*Findings: [F4](#f4), [F5](#f5)*

Two high-severity vulnerabilities affecting UnrealIRCd and Check Point products are now in the Critical Infrastructure Security Enhancement \(KEV\) list with strict federal due dates.

**Recommended actions**
- Apply patches for CVE-2026-114713 and CVE-2026-123039 before the 2026-11-16 deadline.
- Review UnrealIRCd configurations on university networks.
- Update Check Point Firewalls and Management Servers.
